Risk & Transparency

The honest page. Read this before anything else.

Most websites in this space hide this information. We put it up front, because a customer who understands the risks is the only kind worth having.

What can go wrong

The real risks

You can lose everything

Leveraged trading magnifies losses. A small market move can wipe out your deposit, and many retail traders lose money.

No guarantees

A master strategy's past results say nothing certain about the future. Strategies can have long losing periods.

Platform and broker risk

You depend on the broker and the copy platform operating honestly and reliably, including when you want to withdraw. This is why verification matters.

You still carry the responsibility

Copying does not remove your duty to monitor your own account and decide how much to put at risk.

Impersonation scams

Scammers imitate agents and platforms. Never send money to a person's personal account, and only use the official platform links.

Borrowed money is dangerous

Never use loans, rent, school fees, or emergency savings. If losing it would hurt you, do not deposit it.

Check it yourself

How to verify the broker

A website saying it is regulated is not proof. Follow these steps and decide for yourself.

  1. Find what the broker claims

    On the broker's official website, find which regulator and licence number it states. Write both down.

  2. Check the regulator's own register

    Go directly to that regulator's official website and search its public register for the company name and licence number. A genuine licence will appear there. If you cannot find it, treat that as a warning sign.

  3. Read independent coverage, including the critical kind

    Search the broker and the program by name. Independent articles online have raised questions about the broker and the referral structure. Read both the positive and the negative, and note who is saying what.

  4. Ask your agent direct questions

    Ask how withdrawals work, what the fees are, what the worst losing period has been, and what he earns from referrals. A good agent answers plainly.

Our position: reviews and ratings sites are useful, but they are not regulators. Only a regulator's own register can confirm a licence. We would rather you verify and walk away than skip the checks and regret it.
