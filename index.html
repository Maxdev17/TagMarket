Learn first

02

Open your own account

You register an account in your own name through the official sign-up link. Your agent can guide you through the form.

Fund only what you can lose

Deposit only through the broker's official platform, never to an individual's personal account. Start with an amount that would not hurt you if it were lost.

According to X-Fusion, the master strategy's trades are mirrored proportionally on your account. You stay responsible for monitoring your own account.

Set the right expectations

What copy trading is, and is not

It is

  • A way to mirror a strategy's trades on your own account
  • A high-risk activity involving leveraged products
  • Something that requires you to monitor your account

It is not

  • Guaranteed income of any kind
  • Risk-free, or a replacement for savings
  • A reason to borrow money or use rent and school fees
